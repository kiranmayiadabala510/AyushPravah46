DPDP & Privacy-by-Design Architecture

DPDP-Aligned Privacy Demonstration

AyushPravah incorporates privacy-by-design concepts aligned with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 principles:

  • Data Minimization: Only trial-essential clinical & demographic parameters are collected.
  • PII Masking: Patient names are masked to synthetic Trial IDs (e.g. PT-402-BLR) at the UI boundary.
  • Explicit e-Consent: Informed consent capture is mandatory prior to eCRF data entry.
  • Purpose-Based Access: Role-based access control (RBAC) restricts sensitive safety & trial views.

Prototype privacy control — production implementation would enforce server-side access policies.
